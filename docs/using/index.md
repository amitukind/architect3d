# Using the planner

Architect3D draws a home in 2D and lets you walk through it in 3D. It runs
entirely in your browser: there is no account, nothing is uploaded, and there
is no server to upload it to.

That has one consequence worth knowing before you start. **Your designs are kept
in this browser, on this machine.** Clearing your browsing data clears them, and
opening the planner on a different computer will not find them. When a design
matters, [save it as a file or send it as a link](./keeping).

## The three views

The planner shows the same home two ways at once, and you choose how much of
each you want.

| | What you get | Key |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **2D** | The floor plan, filling the window | <kbd>1</kbd> |
| **Split** | Plan on the left, 3D on the right | <kbd>2</kbd> |
| **3D** | The 3D view, filling the window | <kbd>3</kbd> |

In Split you can drag the divider between them, and it moves with the arrow
keys if you would rather not reach for the mouse.

Everything you do in one view shows immediately in the other. Drag a corner on
the plan and the wall moves in 3D while you are still dragging it.

## Where to start

You begin with a four-walled room already drawn, because an empty page is a
worse place to start than a wrong room. Drag its corners until it is the shape
you want, or delete it and draw your own.

If you would rather begin from something furnished, open **Designs** and pick a
starter plan — a studio, a one-, two- or three-bedroom flat, or a duplex — or
one of the two furnished samples.

## Three things people look for first

- **The tour.** The **?** button in the top bar reopens the introduction you saw
  on your first visit, whenever you want it.
- **Every keyboard shortcut**, in one list: press <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>?</kbd>.
- **Undo**, which goes back through everything including a whole design you just
  opened: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd>.
