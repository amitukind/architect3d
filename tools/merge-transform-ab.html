Node transforms: dropped vs honoured

Sprint S4's visual A/B for the one intentional rendering change in the bump. Every version of the item merge before S4 baked each mesh's local matrix, so a mesh sitting under a transformed glTF node was flattened without its parent's transform. mergeMeshes now uses matrixWorld.

Left is the old reading, right is the new one. Both frames share one camera, fitted to the union of the two results, so a difference in size or position is visible rather than normalised away. The grey box is the other side's bounds, drawn for reference.

Only the affected models are listed. Those tagged moved sit under a node that translates or rotates but does not scale: they were drawn at the right size in the wrong place, and there is nothing to weigh up. Those tagged resized change size, and that is the half that needs your judgement.
